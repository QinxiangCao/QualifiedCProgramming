P2569 [SCOI2010] Stock Trading

Description

Recently, lxhgww has gotten into stock investing again. After a period of
observation and learning, he summarized some patterns in stock prices. Over
time, lxhgww predicted the movement of a certain stock for the next T days. On
day i, the buy price per share is AP_i, and the sell price per share is BP_i
(the data guarantees that for every i, AP_i >= BP_i).

However, trading cannot be unrestricted each day, so the exchange stipulates
that in a single buy on day i, you can purchase at most AS_i shares, and in a
single sell on day i, you can sell at most BS_i shares. In addition, the
exchange has two more rules. To avoid excessive trading, the exchange requires
that between any two transactions (a buy or a sell on any day counts as one
transaction), there must be at least W days in between.

That is, if a transaction occurs on day i, then from day i + 1 to day i + W
(inclusive), no transactions are allowed. Also, to prevent monopolies, the
exchange mandates that at any time, the number of shares a person holds cannot
exceed MaxP. Before day 1, lxhgww has a large amount of cash (consider it as
unlimited cash), but no stock. Of course, after T days, lxhgww wants to earn as
much money as possible. Smart programmers, can you help him?

Input Format

The first line of input contains 3 integers: T, MaxP, and W. The next T lines
describe the stock data. The i-th line corresponds to day i and contains 4
integers, namely AP_i, BP_i, AS_i, BS_i.

Output Format

Output a single number: the maximum amount of money lxhgww can earn.

Sample Input

5 2 0
2 1 1 1
2 1 1 1
3 2 1 1
4 3 1 1
5 4 1 1

Sample Output

3

Constraints

- For 30% of the testdata, 0 <= W < T <= 50, 1 <= MaxP <= 50.
- For 50% of the testdata, 0 <= W < T <= 2000, 1 <= MaxP <= 50.
- For 100% of the testdata, 0 <= W < T <= 2000, 1 <= MaxP <= 2000.
- For all testdata, 1 <= BP_i <= AP_i <= 1000,
  1 <= AS_i, BS_i <= MaxP.
