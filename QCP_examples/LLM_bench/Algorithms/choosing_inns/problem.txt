Luogu P1311. Choosing Inns

There are `n` inns arranged from left to right. Inn `i` has a color
`colors[i]` in `[0, k - 1]`, and the coffee shop inside inn `i` has minimum
cost `costs[i]`.

Two tourists want to stay in two different inns with the same color. They will
meet at a coffee shop located between the two chosen inns, inclusive. The plan
is valid if there exists at least one inn between the two chosen positions,
inclusive, whose coffee minimum cost is at most `p`.

Return the number of valid unordered pairs of inns.

Constraints:

- `0 <= n <= 200000`
- `1 <= k <= 50`
- `0 <= p <= 100`
- `0 <= colors[i] < k`
- `0 <= costs[i] <= 100`

Hint:

- Scan from left to right.
- `seen[c]` counts how many inns of color `c` have appeared so far.
- `good[c]` counts how many previously seen inns of color `c` can already form
  a valid pair with a future inn because there is an affordable coffee shop in
  the suffix ending at the current position.
